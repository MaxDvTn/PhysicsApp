# Solar day and sidereal day

Why does the Sun come back to the same point in the sky after 24 hours, while the stars come back after about 23 hours and 56 minutes? The page contains the Year 3 worksheet, in Italian (5 pages, with diagrams) and translated into English, and links to six external simulations to see it in motion.

## How to use it

1. Go through the worksheet: in Italian the five pages can be projected one at a time; in English the text and the diagrams are on one page.
2. Open the simulations at the bottom of the page. The EduMedia one (solar day) is good to start with; to compare the two days use Science Sims or PhysSandbox.
3. With **Stellarium Web** you can look at the sky over Trento 24 hours apart and 23 hours and 56 minutes apart: in the second case the stars are back in the same position.

## Linked simulations

- EduMedia, Solar day
- Science Sims (CCNY), Solar vs Sidereal Day
- PhysSandbox, Sidereal vs Solar Day
- ComPADRE, Sidereal/Solar Day, Naked Eye
- University of Nebraska, Sidereal and Solar Time
- Stellarium Web

The simulations are by other authors and open on their own sites.
